Welcome to Cryptidbama

Welcome to Crypidbama! Here for the wonderfully curious - those who look twice at the shadows that creep along our lush forest floors, the ripples dancing through our lakes, and the mysterious enigmas hiding amidst our glowing coastlines. While local spots such as Wheeler National Wildlife Refuge and Alabama Nature Center will show you the sights and sounds of nature we all know and love, we dive into the Alabama wildlife that mainstream researchers and scientists won’t talk about. We believe that with new species discovered each day, it’s only a matter of time before we stumble across something truly extraordinary in our own backyard.
